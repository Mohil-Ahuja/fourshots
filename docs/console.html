The decision log

Actions only. The sequence numbers skip because the observations that produced them are chained in between — every decline is recorded before it is decided, so a redelivered webhook cannot spend a fifth attempt.

A booked retry is written into the schedule with its 24-hour pre-debit notice and recorded as executed_against_rail: false — Razorpay exposes no call that re-attempts a mandate on a chosen date. An escalation is genuinely executed: a Payment Link raised against the test-mode API. Nothing on this page moves real money.
