fourshots

NPCI allows four attempts per mandate cycle.
This decides how to spend them.

Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026 — Track 03, AI Revenue Recovery

When a UPI AutoPay debit fails, the documented retry policy spends all four attempts on consecutive days — every one of them, in the common case, on a day the account is still empty. The budget is gone before the salary arrives, and the mandate is cancelled. This project treats those four attempts as a budget to allocate under regulatory constraint rather than a loop to run.
